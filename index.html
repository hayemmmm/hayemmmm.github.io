Report to recruit primer

K-Youth Program • Training exercise

From annual report to new-recruit primer

Adapted excerpts from IHH Healthcare's 2024 Annual Report into a business primer for new recruits as part of K-Youth training.

The task

Extract relevant information from a corporate report and reorganize it for readers getting to know the business.

Approach and tools

Used Gemini to extract key information relevant to the primer, then organized the material into a guide for new recruits.

The result

A 14-page primer with nine sections covering company identity, the market landscape, a Business Model Canvas, operations, organizational structure, sustainability, and onboarding resources.

Skills demonstrated

Information extraction, synthesis, business analysis, audience-focused writing, and document organization.

Customer financing brochure

Customer scenario • Brochure design

Three financing plans, one customer recommendation

Created a three-panel home-financing brochure presenting Fixed, Flexi, and Balanced packages, with the Fixed plan selected as the most suitable option for the assigned customer scenario.

The task

Turn the details of three available plans into a customer-facing brochure that explains the recommendation clearly.

Tools and approach

Designed in Canva. AI assisted with the customer recommendation and selection criteria; the brochure design was created without AI.

The design

Organized the brochure around the customer's property goal, a summary of all three packages, and a prominent explanation of the Fixed package. Used contrasting colors, clear headings, and property imagery to guide the reader.

Skills demonstrated

Plan comparison, customer-focused communication, information hierarchy, copywriting, and brochure layout.

Service revenue analysis

Excel • Data analysis and presentation

From service revenue data to strategic findings

Analyzed a dataset of Grab services in Excel, using pivot tables and charts to compare regional performance and service trends. Presented the key takeaways and proposed actions in a separate findings presentation.

The data

2,000 records across six services and four regions, covering Q1 2022 to Q2 2024. The workbook contains the raw data alongside detailed service and regional breakdowns.

Tools and approach

Used Excel for the analysis and Canva for the presentation. AI helped reorganize the existing findings and make the information easier to digest.

The workflow

Raw data → Excel pivot tables → service and regional charts → a 14-page presentation of findings and recommendations.

Selected findings

Across the full dataset, Central contributed 29.6% of revenue and South contributed 28.4%. JustGrab and Grab Plus were the two largest services by total revenue.

Skills demonstrated

Excel pivot tables, data visualization, trend analysis, regional comparison, and communicating analytical findings.
